Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

In short: Panim does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you write stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your snippets, reflections, prayers, topics, favourites, reminder times and settings are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us, and we cannot see them.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. Panim does not use a push notification service, and no information about your reminders leaves your device.

Bible text

The King James Version and the Berean Standard Bible are built into the app and work without a connection.

If you choose a version that is available online only (such as the NKJV, ESV, NASB or NLT), Panim requests the passage or chapter you are reading from Bolls.life, a free online Bible service. That request contains only the passage asked for. As with any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address. Nothing you have written is sent.

App updates

Panim checks Expo's update service for fixes and improvements so that they can reach you without a full App Store update. That check sends technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version, the platform, and a random identifier for this installation. It does not include anything you have written.

Backups

If your phone backs up to iCloud or to your Google account, that backup may include Panim's data. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any snippet or topic in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. On Android, a reinstalled app may restore data from your Google backup; to start fresh, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Children

Panim does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
