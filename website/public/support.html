My reminders aren't arriving

  • Check that notifications are allowed: Panim shows a notice in Settings and on Home when they're off, with a button to turn them on.
  • Check that at least one reminder time is set under Settings → Reminders, and tap Apply after changing them.
  • Open Panim now and then. Reminders are prepared on your phone up to two weeks ahead, and opening the app refills them.

Will I lose my snippets if I change phones?

Panim keeps everything on your device. Your snippets come with you when you restore a new phone from an iCloud or Google backup of the old one. There is no Panim account or cloud sync yet.

Why do some Bible versions need a connection?

The King James Version and the Berean Standard Bible are built into the app and always work offline. Other versions are fetched as you read. Passages you have saved as snippets are stored on your phone and always work offline, whatever the version.

Can I edit or remove the starter snippets?

Yes. They're yours: open any snippet to edit it or delete it, just like the ones you create.
